I lust for one touch
to feel your breath against my neck as you caress my chest
our souls intertwined and our legs interlaced that nothing could break something so divine
I wish to know the myriad of ways in which you taste
to feel the warmth of your embrace as my arms encompass your waist
I want to wander in your eyes and ponder what lies behind
everything I desire, everything I admire
and yet, here I am tied to a divider
trying to decipher
why it is I can’t suppress the lust I possess
for the love I express
