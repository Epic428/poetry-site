every day is the same day
just another day to play it safe
another day to make a wish and not take a risk
if you're hearing this, I failed as a lyricist
lost my mind in chaos lookin for a rhyme I can play off
hoping that just one might take off
it’s time to wake up 

roses are red, violets are blue
I don’t play by the rules and half ass everything I do
I know no emotion because I sold my soul to devotion
heavens lessons for the blank expression of depression
exhaustion’s set in, my head is restless
everything I question
becomes
a do or die suicide for bluer skies
new at five, life passed you by
not because you got high
but because you wouldn’t try
and hid behind a safety net of lies
why?
are you afraid to fly?
are you afraid of life?
are you afraid to die?
you’re already dead inside
like broken dreams
woe is me, I’m so lonely
you know, if only
I think something is wrong
I feel nothing at all
I’ve grown cold to my ambition and I don’t know how I can fix it
listen
I’m at my wits end
excuses are like nooses hanging loose around you
how can I prove to you that you can do everything you set your mind to?
but you pull a little tighter
still afraid that it might hurt
is that really what your time’s worth?
you want a standing ovation
with no motivation to switch your vocation
you need to stop being so frustrated and become innovative
but hurry up, because time’s a wasting
