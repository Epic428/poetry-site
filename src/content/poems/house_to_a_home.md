heavy rain soothes a melancholy mood
I go right, while she goes left
from one hell hole to the next
I try to imagine her dream fueled nightmares
only to witness a beauty so pure and intense
indescribable and without comparison
we’d been catfished by the pursuit of happiness
paper cuts from paper tongues
how it is to be so jealous
locked in a vicarious wanderlust
she’s a diamond in the rough
and to think, she couldn’t be loved
so many things I wish I could say
so many things I wish I could say
to mask the pain, she puts on her face
her contagious smile brightening even my worst days
I want to look into her eyes and see her soul
stand at her side and lend my hand to hold
I want to be the broken piece that makes her whole
show her a love she’s never known
I want to keep her warm when she’s cold
take her from a house to a home
perhaps in another life that may have been possible
a life where I haven’t lost control in the war between my head and my heart
she’s the woman I see buried deep in my dreams
the kind of woman you keep and never leave
