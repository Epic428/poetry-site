Cascades of photons illuminate the gateways to our brain
While our neurotic Faraday disrupts transmissions from reality
Melodically coasting through space,
we savor the melancholy taste of acoustic bruises, contouring the sine waves of emotions
Spelunking in the Fibonacci sequence of life,
we are brooding interlopers casting light in the tunnels of midnight
Through the toxic confusion, the once seemingly innocuous occupants of our introspection become a delicacy for the flesh eating bacteria of our mind
With deep shifts in perspective we impart wisdom as art
and our acute sense of awareness is lost on the obtuse when all that is and ever was, will be
Exhuming the means to an end, derelicts of psychopathy
Bereft of the knowledge we seek, the knowledge to see
We are artists
We are musicians
We are poets
We are human
