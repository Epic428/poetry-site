Am I dreaming?
The glint in her eyes gleaming
And her radiant smile beaming
I must be dreaming, it’s seeming.

Drowning in my thoughts
I cannot escape my head.
Unaware if I’m alive or dead.
All these mixed emotions
Like time is moving in slow motion
Click...Click...Click...Click...

I reach for her cheek
Cupping the back of her head
Unsure if I’ll have the opportunity again
My eyes locked with hers, I pull her closer. 
Chest to chest, face to face
My body trembling, shaking
I should be waking, but I’m not
I’ve transcended to a new place
A world without fear, everything limitless
The chaos surrounding
Faded, no longer clouding
The noises and voices, the echoes of my thoughts
The newfound silence taking their spots
I press my lips against hers
Pure momentary bliss as my heart stops
The universe disappears along with my breath

About to light another smoke
Take another toke off this bowl
Don’t need a bandaid to cover this hole
I don’t want these feelings to go
Because here I sit missing you so
I had a dream and a vision
Just like two stars in a collision
A future so bright, a life fulfilled
Grand experiences and thrills
Making time feel still
Just the two of us never letting go

I snap back to reality
Take another hit of this weed
Everyone wants to talk to me
But all I want is to be free
Free of the noise in my head
Switching my focus to everything said
It’s my time to shine
Make all my dreams come true
And I don’t need you, in this new life of mine
So please don’t make me worry
Take my life and you’ll be sorry
Take your own and everyone will know
You’re now on your own
And I hope you glow
But please don’t try and reach me
You don’t need to teach me
I want nothing from you, I’d give it all to charity
So go fuck yourself!
And leave me alone..

Now that you’re gone I can move on
I have a vision to build on
Dreams to pursue and dreams will come true
Gotta make a list and start something new
Maybe build a house or two
Cuz I’m back with a renewed focus
No more of this hocus pocus
And this bullshit you stole this
Cuz it’ll cost us everything you lost us
And I’mma buy my way to the top
You know I can’t and I won’t be stopped
Try to fuck with me and I’ll call the cops
So get fucked and get lost

This war between my head and my heart
Raging on without a start
But I know what I want and I’m just scared
Like a baby bird pushed from its nest
I don’t know what comes next
I saw a girl in my dreams and suddenly felt blessed
I think she’s the one for me
Oh how true I want that to be
But I’ve been given a blank canvas
A new life teeming with possibilities
Anything I want I can create
Because I know my future will be great
So for the girl in my dreams a picture I will paint

Beneath a winter nights glow
I’ll kiss you under the snow
And by the warmth of a fire
I will fulfill your every desire
I will take you to Paris and Rome
As long as you’re with me we’ll always be home
And for your birthday I’ll give you a rose and a card
The words within always written from the heart
Take a trip with me and let go of reality
Because everything is as it seems
You’re the girl of my dreams
The world won’t feel so heavy 
When you’re with me, no need for anxiety
So let me share with you an opportunity
For you to see that dreams come true
They already do because I found you
So please let me love you
