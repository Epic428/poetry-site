somewhere along the way i lost the answers
somewhere along the way i lost the words
somewhere along the way i forgot all the things to say that would make you stay
some memories remain, while others fade
making me feel strange
making me feel pain
making me feel rage
making me feel shame
leading me astray and making me feel insane
welcome back to the asylum
crescent moon out on the horizon
i can see Hell in your eyes, hun
five years, and nothing between us
im so fucked up, i cant even focus
you know you didn’t have to defend
you know you didn’t have to pretend
and now this is the end
goodbye old friend
