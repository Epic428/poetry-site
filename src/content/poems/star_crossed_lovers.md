you are my suicide, do or die
newer life, beautiful sky
the days are long and grueling
am I confused or delusional, dreaming of you and I?
have you ever wanted to cry, but couldn't because the tears have all run dry, and you didn’t understand why?
welcome to ten years of my life
and yet here I am, unable to remove you from my mind
trying really fucking hard not to regurgitate my overused lines
I’m a piece of shit who really should have died reading between the lines of my fucked up life
so why does it feel like the stars have aligned?
feed me lines to write, be my wife
fuck my racing intrusive thoughts, I shouldn’t drive
count the number of times I carved scars in my heart until they hardened
licking love from a knife until I played my cards right
star-crossed lovers, streams of consciousness, you are everything and I want all of it
my anxiety is a nine out of ten
why do you live rent-free in my head?
angels saved me from death
when you and I met and you took my breath
as I reinvent myself, again
