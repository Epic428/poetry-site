I’m three smokes deep, sitting in the car alone
I suppose it’s all I’ve ever known
I’m sensitive in every sense of the word
send shivers all through my spine
the tears only ever run dry
I take five to ride this high
or must be this high to ride
or ride this high to a take five
I’m.. confused, and I think you know why
like that time I found the perfect rhyme
across the sapphire glass my thumb would glide
each syllable prancing about in my mind
a page filled with life and left to die

tell me I’m blind
tell me I should read between the lines
tell me my fears
tell me it’s like we were never here
tell me I’m mean
tell me how unlucky you are to have me
tell me why, I taste blood-wine honey and lies
tell me that I’m playing a wicked game,
the world was on fire and no one could save me but you
it’s strange what desire will make foolish people do

I’m three smokes deep, sitting in the car alone
though I suppose it’s all I’ve ever known
I never knew that magic could be so painful
recall the time I was saved by an angel
burned by the glow of her halo
and left with the mark of betrayal
eliciting various forms, she often visits
playing my heart like a goddamn fiddle 
I found solace in a stranger
because deep down I fuckin’ hate her
bittersweet seems the melody I dream
each note secured in the staccato of a multifaceted ring
polished behind the vibrato of a white gold home
and cut by the harmony of a princess
there’s been a sudden shift to indifference
it’s got me confused about the blatant ignorance
while looming behind, I can feel her presence
it is imminent
an angel should pay a visit
with her old friend, disappointment
is this another page filled with life and left to die?
because if it is, then this, is my goodbye
