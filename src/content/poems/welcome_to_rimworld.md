Randy Random wasn’t always a madman
When he crashlanded the tribals went savage
They switched his leg for a peg and sent him on a mental break
Then he slapped a bear in a show of dominance and now all his limbs are made of bionics
His tame failed all because he ate without a table

One summer evening, high on smokeleaf, up came a spelopede and some insect jelly
The psychoid tea really had him thinking he’s crazy because next thing he’s fighting a scyther biker and its pet centipede

While Phoebe and Cassandra chose to chillax and watch some classics, pirates captured his sister and took her prisoner
Because the ice sheet had no animals, he turned into a cannibal
So he sold some devilstrand to a muffalo caravan and came back with a Psychic Insanity Lance
And soon they felt the wrath of a psychopath as one by one they became leather hats

Welcome to Rimworld where wild stories unfold
