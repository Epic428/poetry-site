love is a drug
an addiction for the afflicted
a vision for the gifted
 
I was standing at the edge of death
remembering everything she ever said
wondering if I would be taking my last breath
would it be a decision I’d regret?
was it all in my head?
what do I keep staring at these letters for?
just to write another metaphor about the end I’m headed toward?
I write random lines in the confines of my scrambled mind about a fragile life that keeps passing by
like a visionary I’m playing Pictionary with the dictionary
a literary obituary built squarely upon skeletal remains and bloodstains
the page is my sanctuary highlighting and flaunting all of my haunting mistakes 
echoing my brain that from which I cannot escape like the cage that ensnares me
barely free, I live carefree and yet fear is what scares me
scared to see a scarcity of dreams except my nightmares are right where I left them
A prisoner of my mind locked in toxic logic 
I’d become the product of my despair and beyond repair, the shadow of my former dreams
I attempt to believe and tapped into reality
how tragic an epiphanic maniac would conceive that I’m the one who holds the key
I hold the key...
I hold the key
to break free of these chains and build out my legacy

like a starving artist I carved scars in my heart until they hardened
I was feeding off the garden of eden like a heathen and justified every reason why
the grass is always greener until you’re staring the grim reaper in the eye
but death stands beside
because hidden behind this fake smile of mine hides a faded child inside
are we getting too old?
have we fallen for fools gold?
withering like a rose this is the bitter life I chose
make me an angel and save me from this fable
I know what I bring to the table
and for too long have I sipped from the ladle
I bought stock in pot ‘cause I’d invested in pestilence
and with a monolith of confidence erected, I dissected benevolence
and manifested a trifecta of questions infested with elegance which hold evidence of an intrepid perfection affected by my perception
 
we rise and we fall like Autumn’s last call
we change our colors and fight with each other until we’re lying in the gutter
dead in the street like a pile of leaves 
misery loves company so I was just wondering
will you come with me?
