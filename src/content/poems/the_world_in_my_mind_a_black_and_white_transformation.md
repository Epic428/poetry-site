Cracks of light seeping through the surface
Illuminating every reason not to be nervous
Wandering aimlessly searching for a purpose
Abstracted in a world devoid of color
Dissociative in chaos and disorder
A lifeless body wrought with temptation
Burning desires for a sense of liberation
Gasping to insufflate the lungs from near death suffocation
A succubus eviscerating every last drop of life
Disillusion fueling endless streams of strife
Consumed by darkness, enveloped by fear
Vision obscured making the path forward unclear
A wasteland of destitution locked in perpetuity
A connection of two souls lacking in continuity
The hostility of living in this temple 
Making death seemingly the inevitable
Descending deeper into oblivion
Empty and isolated, overwhelmed with confusion
Deprived of happiness and tormented by greed
Scars fabricated into a heart that can no longer bleed
A decrepit soul left to wither and a mind lost to atrophy
A drone surviving alone stripped of a home
Forced to gather the pieces of a broken puzzle
A state of depression destined to crumble

Rising from the burning ashes of the aftermath
A phoenix bursts into a show of flames and highlights a new path
All that remains is a ghost in the shell
A specter left to rot in this hell
The lifeless body transcends into a new being
The evidence of a new life made promising and believing
Guided by the stars and destined for greatness
A heart made courageous and a soul no longer faceless
A name that will one day be famous
Color bleeds into the world around
An exhilarating sense of freedom abound
An angel breathes new life and provides hope
The comforting release of pressure of the world
And the severance of a vampiric umbilical cord
For upon me is the dawn of a new time
This is the world in my mind
A black and white transformation
