Have you ever wondered
What the first light was like?
The Big Bang that created it all
The atoms and molecules swimming in chaos
Racing and floating through an empty void
Cosmic dust shaping and constructing
Everything we touch
We see. We hear. We taste. We smell. 
Life as we know it being born.

That’s what I imagine
The light at the end of a tunnel to be like
The dawn of something new and incredible
A journey to the center of the earth
Scaling the side of a mountain
Over a lush, jade forest
The air of a cool breeze
Juxtaposed to the intense heat of the sun

The tunnel I’d been caged in
The eternally lasting darkness befallen upon me
Dissipating like the mist on a lake one summer morning
Muffling the echoes of my thoughts
The sounds of a new day

And as the tunnel draws to a close
And a new life begins
But what do I see standing in front of me
An angel bestowed from the heavens
The aura of happiness radiating
As intensely as the smile of a child
On Christmas morning
The gift of the cure for cancer
The everlasting flame of a raging fever

This angel, she whispers “I want you”
As she nuzzles my neck
My heart racing with the ferocity of a stampede
The flutters in my stomach like an endless rollercoaster
My face cramping from all the smiling
My cheeks adorned with rubies
And my head as clear as the sky following a storm
“I want you, too” I whisper back
