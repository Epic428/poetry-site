I felt all alone
Like living inside a hole
I felt like a mannequin
So full of life but not really living
To lie is to not try, unless you’re trying to lie
But all I ever thought about was how I wanted to die
And how I wanted to put on my wings and fly
But all I could do instead was cry
Because you didn’t even try
And you spit out more lies
You destroyed our lives
It took me two years to realize
So I spent my days trying to fantasize
About a life outside the walls of my mind
Trying to silence the hallowed voices taking my time
Not ever really knowing all the right pieces would fall in line

As I looked in the mirror
I saw blood stained tears run down my face
It couldn’t have been any clearer
I had to get out of that hellish place
Failure and death were in my reflection
Because of your infection
And our quantum disconnection
Our lives didn’t need the complexion
So I learned to swallow my pride
But always being afraid to hide
Never really knowing why I wanted to die
Never telling you how much I cried
And never learning the reason why
Two years of my life all alone
Not living but rather existing in a broken home
