i listen to the acoustic bruises strewn through music as hallow sounds hollowed out from a soul bound depression
an intelligent inception of deafening deception begging me to question my perspective
the empty reflections cut to mirror consciousness as ominous intentions
whimsical subliminal syllables fill the holes with spotted bliss
ignorance derived from lies deprived of life and thrown like knives

I’d like to sign a petition
to decommission my mind that’s been conditioned
conditioned by people who wouldn’t listen when I told them I didn’t fit in
conditioned by addictions hidden within the wisdom of my prism
conditioned by prescriptions fed from physicians the system had given
conditioned by wicked bitches more concerned with strippin than commitments
conditioned by the dip shit delinquent who won’t be convicted of pilfering my distinction
conditioned by a malignant mother as an infant misfit to listen this instant
crying and vying for attention
but the adolescence of teenage depression
confused and reckless abused and restless
twisted up in a lovers affection that I couldn’t have sex with hangs its head in gifted brilliance
divorced by the absence of passion
deformed by the quickness of death
devoured by the sour power of authorities informing me I’m being forced to conformity 
enormously distorted by abnormally flawed laws invented by anomalous gods synonymous with cops
the dogs of the neighborhood watch

fuck!
I just want to scream!

it’s clear to see, clear to me, this world wasn’t built for me
my dreams, buried deep, beneath a sea of greed
leaving me to question what I believe
monopoly wannabe’s want me
to have a college degree
to escape the monotony of routines
but my body’s autonomy is costing me
my ability to be free that it’s viciously killing me
money is the ultimate symbol of status
love, drugs, and politics, money buys all of it
except my happiness
love, drugs, and politics, money buys all of it
except my happiness
except my happiness reflects my blasphemous poetry about the system that’s controllin’ me
or perhaps it is my blasphemous poetry that is reflected by my lack of happiness?
infectious, my brain is in a frenzy like diseases that are deadly
conditionally conflicted and heavily scripted
I’d like to sign a petition
to decommission my mind that’s been conditioned
conditioned by fictitious pictures painted with vivid descriptions typically in the form of religious scriptures which depict and inflict  auspicious visions positioned by ravenous greed and miraculous deceit
I repeat
I’d like to sign a petition
to decommission my mind that has been conditioned 
conditioned
by the American Dream
