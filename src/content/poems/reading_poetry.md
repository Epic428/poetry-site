I’m hittin up a read like it’s a meet and greet
but I’m just doin me and sharing some poetry
Letting out my feelings for the world to see
some of the shit I say you wouldn’t believe
But it’s true, it happened to me and now I’m setting it free
Oh my god, what a relief to finally find some peace
like comin to a clearing between the trees
All these writers are inspiring me to get up and read and keep chasin my dreams
But I keep sharing stories about a hero who doesn’t get fame or glory
cause the alternative is just too boring
I’d leave everyone snoring and asking for more, but I won’t be ignored
I’ll show you everything I’ve learned and let my voice be heard

we all just wanna create
we all just wanna relate
we all just wanna be great
we all must wanna be fake

Listen to me slowly, my mouth’s echoing globally
rhythm and poetry, don’t come up actin like you know me
playin god like it’s our job, actin confident like we on top of it
you don’t know what you have until you lost it
take the ball don’t drop it
stop it, start it, smoke it til you get retarded
here today, gone tomorrow
always blurring the line before crossing over
say what you need to say before it’s too late
take it day by day and live for today
cause the storms will pass
and even though you know there’s no goin back
there’s still questions left to ask
experiences left to have
