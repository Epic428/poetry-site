I dug my own grave
and I think I fell in deep
hellish dreams keep me awake
last night I couldn’t sleep
see, some days feel like one in the same
I wake up in pain, I don’t eat, I forget to brush my teeth
rinse and repeat, like a broken record skippin a beat
I embraced change only to become afraid
afraid of all my mistakes
afraid of what I could create
afraid of being erased
And as I lie, haunted, by a constant barrage of comments gossiped about by the entourage of goblins buried in my conscience
the cost of my problems go beyond the skeletons in the closet that won’t stop talkin
cold hearts and logic controlled by old scars and toxins
gold stars and nonsense too complex to process
so I push limits and test boundaries
I blur the line before crossing over
I’m like a ghost nobody knows and blend between
a chameleon in a sea of seven billion
packed in the duffel bag strapped to my back I’m carryin the carrion of my dreams
careless and free, yet, tearin apart at the seams
and I don’t care enough to scream when anxiety got me fucked like post nut clarity
so, um, spare me from the aromatherapy of the bullshit you’re circulating in the air above me
because now I see, I must be one twisted individual, an imbecile, for attempting to decrypt every one of your mixed signals
to speak in the literal
to dream in the figurative
to believe in the mystical
so I smile and wave as I keep digging my grave
