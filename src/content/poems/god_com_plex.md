i am the devil, she is the angel, anger and betrayal
hanging from my halo, I AM THE ONLY GOD I PRAY TO
fortifying barriers few are able to break through
i blink from the eye of one tempest to the next, enveloped by chaos
unable to wake up, i weave dreams between the seams of the tapestry of time from a fabric of lies and see a sadness in her eyes as tragic as mine
trapped, half alive, while catfished by catfish eyes that capitalize capital I, then act surprised when cast aside like capital WHY
out of line, defiant, divided dandelions dance across fields of familiarity and mindless meadows sowing seeds deep beneath the dreaming tree, each repeatedly screaming for peace
as you stand face to face with the faceless, confused and angry, waiting to go apeshit
remember, i literally litter the pages with a million little alliterations and illustrate to you illiterate apes the myriad of ways in which i am the greatest
