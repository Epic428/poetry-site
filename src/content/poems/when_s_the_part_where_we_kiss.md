I licked love off a knife and the taste was bittersweet
they say one day it’ll get easy
it’s New Year’s Eve, I’m sat in the shower drowning some tears
the mirror reflecting the old, I ask myself what happened
how is it to be a hopeless romantic
a fallen angel formed from passion
I’d found solace in a stranger while it was snowing paper
standing in the back of this machine she told me it was like a Hallmark movie
she opened me like a book, scribbling notes in the margin
cutting through the cartilage where it’s the hardest 
they say home is where the heart is
home is wherever she is
God, if you’re listening, 
tell me when’s the part where we kiss?
