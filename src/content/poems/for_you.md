I know it isn’t much 
and it’s obviously not nearly enough
but I know what it’s like when times are tough and life is rough
I don’t need the funds and you have kids you love
I’d do more if I could because you stand where I once stood
so I hope this helps and you focus on yourself
keep your head held high and let the stars of the sky be your guide
you deserve to have a beautiful life
the same as I deserve to have mine
I know you’ll be fine
