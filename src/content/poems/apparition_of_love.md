tell you I feel a change in the air
after I gave you my heart to borrow
you tell me all this just ain’t fair
say I’ll see you tomorrow
I gotta get through this pain and sorrow

blood-wine honey and lies
I licked love from the knife which severed my spine
crossed my heart and hoped to die
promoted to the afterlife
I hear the call from the other side

close enough to touch
real enough to touch
it’s hard for me to let go
like watching the sun explode
the light disappearing in slow-mo
