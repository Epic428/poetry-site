I’m haunted by ghosts the world will never know
a restless soul in a demented hole to the devil I’d been sold
in twisted games I played, I’d been dealt a hand of fate much too difficult to escape
dreams masquerade a nightmares take
a high stakes poker face comes in spades
and while my minds awake, hearts debate
in troubled times I roll the dice and live a double life
snake eyes play nice and say lies
so I paid a high price with my sacrifice only to win a fake prize
with swallowed pride held behind hollow eyes,  I tell a tale as cold as ice
stung by trust, twisted tongues spit and cut
a foolish love stuck in wanderlust
when the sun sets and darkness rests
I wonder what comes next in your game of Russian roulette?
