the key to my heart is rusted and falling apart
there’s dust on the dresser and walls filled with cheap art
I wish upon a star and wish that things aren’t the way they are
I wish you could see, beyond the scars and all your broken parts, to see the light through the dark
two worlds apart, the pendulum swings
my abuser peddles venom as dreams
while the media tell tales of hell on the street
you’re flippin’ through the pages of my patience
curatin’ my thoughts and where they take us
slip in the spaces provided, to find a fire rages inside 
she leaves me blind while time ticks by
a vixen to scribe, when heaven and earth collide
she made love to a machine
a queen, lustfully serene
look into her lapis eyes
and pause… just to breathe
