Baby, you need to know that I’m scared. Scared of what’s to come
Scared of the life I thought I was running from
But it’s the life I’m running to that’s got me afraid
Of course it’ll be great, and I’m not sayin you shouldn’t stay
But the thing I’m most scared of is what happens to the people you love
The worry and fear of trying to survive in a world so cold
The thoughts and feelings that one day I might not be enough
You and I, we fit like a glove, you fell into my arms from above
And when you’re not around I miss you so much
I miss your touch, your taste, your smell
So much I want to share and so much I want to tell
Still recovering from when I fell head over heels
I caught these feels and I’ll never let go
I’m tryin to keep it real but I still want you to know

I’m scared of being hurt. Scared of having my heart broken
I’ve been hurt before but this time I think I’m sure
I’ve found the one, someone I can trust, someone I can truly love
Someone, for whom, truer words were never spoken 

Baby I’m scared. Scared for the life that we share
Scared of holding on and scared of letting go
You’re the love of my life,
The kind of girl I hope to one day have as my wife
You’re the woman I hope to never lose
The woman I will always choose

I remember when life was simple like country living
