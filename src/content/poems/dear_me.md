dear me

don’t be the victim of a bully
you still have your dreams, while they lost theirs
I know you’re scared because I’ve been there,
but don’t be afraid, everything will be okay
it won’t be easy, I know
and yeah, you could still fail, I suppose
but you have to leave your comfort zone
I know you want to be comfortable and that’s certainly understandable
but that feeling of comfort is what’s going to keep you under

dear me
don’t be the victim of anxiety
when you look into the darkness and all you see
are the silhouettes of dreams eclipsed by reality
just remember you only need to turn on the light
forget about the symmetry of black and white, what is wrong and what is right
the story is yours to write

dear me
don’t be a victim of yourself
you create your hell
sure you can’t change the pain you felt
but you can play the hand you’re dealt
so let the acoustic bruises be your music
and sting like a bumble bee on humble streets
because you can’t have doubts when you fall from the clouds that your feet won’t land on the ground
