look at you
lying in the ashes of your filth
burning in a glass house surrounded by those who came before
you’re the butt of every joke and cancerous to the soul
a temporary muse, used and abused
evanescently wavering from white to black only to be discarded in the trash
I can’t breathe around you, but I can’t breathe without you
and since that first taste when I felt the rush in my veins, you just haven’t been the same
but like an undead whore you keep coming back for more and I can’t seem to put you down
this is my life now
tomorrow I’ll light you up, knowing you’re only a crush
