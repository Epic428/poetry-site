come, let’s get high tonight and I’ll open your mind
allow me to strip away any semblance of entitlement and take you on a journey of enlightenment
follow me into my dreams and I’ll show you the world isn’t as it seems
fueled by hate and greed, they take from me
wondering when I’ll be free, when I’ll find my peace
and when you grow up running the streets feeding off government cheese
unable to breathe because you couldn’t afford some heat
then you’ll know what it means to wanna be free
imagine waking inside the world of your imagination and thinking it’s a safe place
only to find that it’s filled with strange faces
a world that uses violence to silence the voices of the brightest and poisons your mind, yet
they say you get what you give but the system is fuckin rigged
they designed the life that you live
the system is twisted by a media that forces you to listen
while people are staring at the church steeple like it’s a needle
praying their new religion doesn’t cloud their vision
with their gods hidden behind anonymity in the land of liberty
enriching their well encrypted self interest
they’ll attempt to arrest the feelings in your chest if you should question them
oblivious to the smoke and mirrors in a simulation programmed by fuhrers
welcome to the matrix
here’s a pill, take this
to go alone would be dangerous
nameless, a man of the faceless
I’ll be your agent, a link between worlds
welcome to the fourth dimension
the new world order, is it hell or heaven?
you walled your border, purgatory descended
dimentia defended furor dissension war dependent
nail biters and frail fighters
pacifist masochists, you haven’t heard the last of this
from trap artists to anarchists
the Dalai Lama to the Jolly Roger
cookbooks for hood crooks
if you close your eyes and open your mind you’ll see how the world could look
