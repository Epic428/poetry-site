and the mind is numb
stuck, in the purgatory of what it has become
and you’re drawing me closer
yeah you’re drawing me closer
closer to my goal
and you’re holding the door open
yeah you’re holding the door open
waiting for me to walk through
yet I walk alone
stoned with ghosts and riddled with fear, I walk alone
with my heart on my sleeve and my dreams held near
I walk alone,
wasting time
skating by
staying high
chasing my
waking life
and the mind is numb
stuck, in the purgatory of what it has become
oblivious to your love, and blinded by delusion
useless and gruesome, and twisted with confusion
life is just an illusion
a mirage
a facade
and here in our minds we play God
changing seasons
chasing demons
does it ever become
easy?
the clouds are bleeding and I’m six feet sleeping
staring at the ceiling deeply dreaming
please keep me breathing
every reason to be here is fleeting
my mind is filled with scattered transient reflections of its fractured ambient dimensions
I know the pieces fit, because I watched them fall away
mildewed and smoldering
and my thesis is, that each has their own place
spilled blood and poetry
we keep em in a black box, never to be seen like Sasquatch
and even though we know it’s a bad spot, we stay good until the last drop
strokin the boner of closure
tryna get off this emotional roller coaster 
throwin salt over our shoulder
and as the world grows colder and colder
the highs and lows, the twists and turns
the butterflies in my stomach churn
and the mind is numb
stuck, in the purgatory of what it has become
but I don’t give a fuck,
I’m done
