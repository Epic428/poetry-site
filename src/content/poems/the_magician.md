satirical miracles, smoke and mirrors,
magicians make the best politicians
sleight of hand to buy a slice of land
cries the mice of man
follow the leader, follow the money
sweeter than honey and deeper when bloody
down the rabbit hole we go
back and forth playing whack-a-mole
grab a pack of smokes and pack a bowl
the irrational animals intangible poison paranoia employs a radical voyage
a blind man believes what he sees
a wise man sees what he believes
open eyes, open wide, open mind
broken time, stolen blind, spoken wise
unrivaled lies redefine phantom lines and stifled crimes for a ruse
a game of life you’re destined to lose
disillusioned and useless
an assassin of question, last weeks contestant another acquiescent lemming
the sands of time highlight our past in the eyes of the hourglass
why must we oblige?
tethered to the divide
for the better of mankind
