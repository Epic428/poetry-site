I’d grown too attached, become too accustomed
I wish we could go back, to when it was just us
it’s the hardest pill I’ve had to swallow
there were things left I wanted to tell,
things left I wanted to show
I tried to call, but I put down my phone
I slept facing the wall, crying alone
spoon feeding myself another lie
that everything will be alright
I licked love off a knife
I spilled my heart on the floor
the hand is wringing the towel dry
and I’ve been left to drown
mopping up the remnants
feeling helpless, breathless
