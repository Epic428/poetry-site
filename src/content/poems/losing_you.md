as I weep beneath the dreaming tree
my head and my heart wage wars with nightmares dressed as daydreams
the demons, they play, chain-smoking cigarettes to stay awake
the ground decorated by a million maple seeds
the floors perpetually dirty and the boards they squeak
my bones are cold and my chest is filled with holes
she’s the woman I see buried deep in my dreams
the kind of woman you keep and never leave
she’s just a friend, yet she lives rent-free in my head
running through my emotions and I’m out of breath
unsure if I feel alive or dead, disconnected
I solve problems all day but don’t solve my own
I think a lot of thoughts, but maybe I’ve been thinking all wrong
I didn’t think I could feel as much pain as I do
when faced with the thought of losing you
when you know, you know
you take a piece of me with you
