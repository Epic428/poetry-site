Look, I’m an insecure person 
I just don’t think that I’m worth it
When I’m with you I get nervous
Despite what you see on the surface
I have issues and i don’t need a box of tissues
But the issue is I think I’m kinda useless
Even though I seem to have confidence and can be a bit cocky
I live my life rather sloppy this is my consequence
I usually live in my head and sometimes I wish I was dead
I live with my depression and I have an obsession
I wanna make people happy cause I wish that was me
I’ve had a rough life and I grew up on the street
Almost made a girl my wife instead I got beat
I have dreams and goals and my hearts filled with holes
I know what I want but my life is fraught
Love me or love me not just know that I’m flawed
My head and my heart are twisted in knots
Now that I’m single I have the ability to mingle
But my life is like a riddle and you’re like a puzzle
My honesty always gets me in trouble
And I wish I had you to snuggle
But I have to give you some space
Give you time to clear your minds 
When you’re ready just tell me the time and place
The world gets heavy when you carry it on your shoulder
It’s just a big rock a really big boulder
I’ll probably still carry it when I’m older
I fear for my sister and I wish he didn’t hit her
I care for my brother and I hate my biological mother
I respect my father and hope I don’t read his name on the blotter
My life is rife with drama I just wanna sit in the sauna
I’ve made a lot of mistakes I have no regrets
I’ve done quite a bit of drugs I could go for some X
This is my interlude sittin in a swimming pool on an inner tube
I love the summer but I wasted three and that was a bummer
Floatin down the lazy river sometimes I act kinda bitter
I went to my old home, she said she has a brain tumor
She must have a wicked sense of humor
Cause when I opened the envelope I wondered why I had hope
Now she’ll probably die alone
This is my stream of consciousness a life full of consequences
I wasted my time at school cause I thought I was cool
But now I feel like fool cause I don’t play by the rules
I want someone who can deal with my insecurities
Someone who will love me for my impurities
I want someone who thinks I’m flawless
Because I would treat her like a goddess
I act kinda quirky and usually I look kinda smirky
But lately I’ve been feelin kinda flirty at least I’m only thirty
I’m a little hungry and kinda thirsty
I wanna buy your first drink what do you think
You look good in pink and I’m the weakest link
I need to switch up my focus and take time for myself
Like an elf on the shelf I’m in a new place everyday
I’ll just let the universe show us the way
I won’t go in reverse I’ll just keep moving forward
I have dreams and goals that I’m working toward
If I pick the wrong direction I might end up in a hearse
But where I’m sittin right now things could be a lot worse
It’s time I end this as these will be the last lines that I write
Once I send this I’ll need more inspiration to ignite
There’s a fire burning in my heart I know I’ll be alright
Please don’t worry I don’t think you could ever hurt me
