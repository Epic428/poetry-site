I’m a comedian chameleon
a repetition reptilian
obliteratin’ alliteration liberation
figure skatin a new configuration for inspiration
regurgitatin’ figure eights like the Winter Games
I eviscerate the beginner phase until winner takes
and incinerate dinner plates like minute steaks
to awaken rhythmic vibrations gyratin’ my mind lately
cigarettes fade and regrets a blaze
my mind hasn’t changed
self restraint from senseless rage
oh, the games we play
lessons of the grave
Heaven sets the stage
