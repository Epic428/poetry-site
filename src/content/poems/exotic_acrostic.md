Ashes lie beneath the remains of our past
Serendipitously, our souls intertwine, dancing as one 
Happiness just is, too
Lost souls swimming in a fish bowl
Ecstasy coursing through the abyss
Years become decades

Interloping between blurred lines

Looking through the glass
Opening our minds 
Vividly blind
Escaping

You only live once, I say, as we meet beneath
Obsidian skies lit by diamonds
United by the Divine
